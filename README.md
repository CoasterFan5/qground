# qground
Play with both classical gates and quantum gates in an interactive 2d Canvas, and share them with others. 

## Classic
Get started at `/play/classic` where you can place both charged items, non-charged items, connectors, not gates, and gates, or gates, and of course, an xor gate.

Hit play and see what happens to an 8 segment display, and a small led. 

## Quantum 
Move on to quantum gates, at `/play/quantum` where you can do the same thing but with quantum gates. No fun lights to play with but you can measure!
